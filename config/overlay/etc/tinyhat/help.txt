Tiny Hat

Alt+Space	Menu (also the Applications button or right-click)
Ctrl+Alt+T	Terminal
Ctrl+Alt+P	Program in C in Orbiton (2x Ctrl+Space to run)
Ctrl+Alt+I	Program a game in Fennel and LÖVE (2x Ctrl+Space to run)
Ctrl+Alt+F	Web browser
Ctrl+Alt+S	ScummVM
Ctrl+Alt+D	DOSBox-X
Alt+Shift+W	Close a window (Alt+F4 also works)
Alt+Shift+F	Toggle fullscreen
Alt+Enter	Toggle fullscreen in ScummVM and DOSBox-X
Alt+Tab	Switch between windows
Ctrl+Alt+Up / Down	Volume
Print	Screenshot to ~/Pictures
Ctrl+Alt+L	Lock the screen
Ctrl+Alt+H	Hide this help (press again to show it)

USB sticks and CDs show up in /media and as D: in DOSBox-X.
Put DOS games in ~/DOS, which is C: in DOSBox-X.
A hard disk in this PC with DOS on it becomes C: and ~/DOS becomes E:.
Flight of the Amazon Queen is ready to play in ScummVM.
