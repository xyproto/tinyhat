Tiny Hat

Alt+Space	Application Menu
Alt+Shift+W	Close a window (Alt+F4 also works)
Alt+Tab	Jump between windows
Alt+Shift+F	Toggle fullscreen
Alt+Enter	Toggle fullscreen in ScummVM, DOSBox-X and ZSNES 2
Ctrl+Alt+Up / Down	Volume

Ctrl+Alt+D	Launch DOSBox-X
Ctrl+Alt+F	Web browser
Ctrl+Alt+K	Keyboard layout selection
Ctrl+Alt+L	Lock the screen
Ctrl+Alt+I	Example game in Fennel and LÖVE (2x Ctrl+Space to run)
Ctrl+Alt+P	Example game in C (2x Ctrl+Space to run)
Ctrl+Alt+S	ScummVM
Ctrl+Alt+T	Terminal
Ctrl+Alt+Z	ZSNES 2

Right click desktop	Toggle background
PrtSc	Screenshot to ~/Pictures

USB sticks and CDs show up in /media and as D: in DOSBox-X.
Put DOS games in ~/DOS, which is C: in DOSBox-X.
A hard disk in this PC with DOS on it becomes C: and ~/DOS becomes E:.
