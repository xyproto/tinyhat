Alt+Shift+F	Toggle fullscreen
Alt+Enter	Toggle fullscreen in ScummVM, DOSBox-X and ZSNES 2
Ctrl+Alt+Up / Down	Audio volume

Ctrl+Alt+B	Bluetooth devices

Ctrl+Alt+D	Launch DOSBox-X
Ctrl+Alt+F	Example game in Fennel and LÖVE (2x Ctrl+Space to run)
Ctrl+Alt+G	Example GBA program in C (2x Ctrl+Space to run)
Ctrl+Alt+H	Toggle sharing
Ctrl+Alt+I	LibreSprite
Ctrl+Alt+K	Keyboard layout selection
Ctrl+Alt+L	Lock the screen
Ctrl+Alt+M	mtPaint
Ctrl+Alt+N	Netsurf web browser
Ctrl+Alt+O	DOS programming (Open Watcom)
Ctrl+Alt+P	Example point-and-click game in Lua (2x Ctrl+Space to run)
Ctrl+Alt+Q	Flight of the Amazon Queen
Ctrl+Alt+S	ScummVM
Ctrl+Alt+T / Ctrl+Alt+R	Terminal
Ctrl+Alt+W	Write a book in Orbiton
Ctrl+Alt+X	256 byte demoscene intro (2x Ctrl+Space to run)
Ctrl+Alt+Z	ZSNES 2

Right click desktop	Toggle background
PrtSc	Screenshot to ~/Pictures
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q	Power off
Ctrl+Alt+Del	Reboot

USB sticks and CDs show up in /media and as D: in DOSBox-X.
Put DOS games in ~/DOS, which is C: in DOSBox-X.
A hard disk in this PC with DOS on it becomes C: and ~/DOS becomes E:.
